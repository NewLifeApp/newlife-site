30 Day Reset

A guided month to build a habit or break one.

One short lesson, one small move and one honest check-in a day, for thirty days. Plus a tool for the hard moments. It is optional, and it never takes over the rest of the app.

A day in the plan

Small steps. Every day.

  • A lesson. A few plain sentences about how habits work, and a question to think about.
  • A move. One specific thing to do today, built around your habit.
  • A check-in. Mark the day honestly. When building: I did it, a floor version, or not today. When breaking: clear today, or I slipped.
  • A tool for hard moments. A 90-second urge timer with a breathing sun when you are breaking a habit, or a two-minute start when you are building one.

Your thirty days sit in one grid. Past days stay open, so a late check-in is always possible.

Choose your track

Pick the closest. Make it yours.

Every track comes with thirty concrete daily moves. There is also a "something else" option for any habit you want to build or stop.

🌱 Build a habit

Start something that sticks.

  • 🏃 Move your bodyA daily movement habit that survives bad days.
  • 🌙 Sleep betterA steadier body clock and a calmer evening.
  • 📚 Read every dayTen quiet minutes with a book instead of a screen.
  • 🧘 Calm & breatheA few minutes of stillness, every day.
  • 🌅 Own your morningsA simple first hour that sets up the day.
  • 🥗 Eat & drink wellSmall, steady food habits without a diet.
  • 🎯 Learn a skillDaily practice at something you want to get good at.
  • 💬 Reach out to peopleOne small act of connection a day.

🚫 Break a habit

Take back control of something that takes too much.

  • 🚭 NicotineCigarettes, vapes, pouches.
  • 🍷 AlcoholStop drinking, or hold to a clear limit.
  • 🌿 CannabisTake a real break.
  • 📵 PornGet back control of your time and attention.
  • 🎲 GamblingBetting, casino, slots, sports, apps.
  • 📱 Phone & social mediaScrolling, doomscrolling, endless feeds.
  • 🎮 GamingGames that take more time than you meant.
  • 🍫 Sugar & junk foodFewer sweets, sodas and snacks, without a diet.
How the thirty days run

Four weeks, one arc.

Each plan follows a four-week arc, so the lessons build on each other.

🌱 Building a habit

Week 1Start tiny

Make it so small you can’t fail.

Week 2Make it routine

Anchor it to your day. Don’t level up yet.

Week 3Handle the dips

The messy middle: boredom, bad days, real life.

Week 4Make it yours

Identity, a maintenance level, and a plan for slips.

🚫 Breaking a habit

Week 1Clear the decks

Decide, make it hard to reach, protect your basics.

Week 2Rewire the routine

Map triggers, trade don’t just stop, ride the urges.

Week 3Stress-test

Risky situations, feelings underneath, the slip plan.

Week 4Lock it in

Who you’re becoming, early warnings, life after 30.

Your plan, your words

Set up in a minute.

Write your rule

Something you can answer yes or no to every day, like "No phone after 10pm". Ideas are one tap away.

Say why

Optional. Write it in your own words. It is what you'll read on a hard night.

Start

Begin today or tomorrow. You can leave the Reset at any time with the X in the corner.

Please read: this is a self-guided plan

The 30 Day Reset is a self-improvement tool. It is not medical advice, therapy or treatment, and it does not replace a doctor or counsellor.

  • Some habits can be dangerous to stop suddenly. If you drink heavily or use substances your body depends on, talk to a doctor before you stop.
  • The urge timer and the help numbers are always free to open, even before you subscribe.
  • In the United States: call or text 988 if you are in crisis. SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357. Quitline for tobacco: 1-800-QUIT-NOW. Problem gambling: 1-800-MY-RESET.
  • Everything you write stays on your phone.
