Common questions

How do I add a widget? Press and hold your Home Screen, tap +, search for NewLife and add the streak or today's-habits widget. Open the app once so it can fill in.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then NewLife.

How do I delete my data? NewLife stores everything on your phone, so deleting the app deletes your data.

My streak looks wrong. Streaks are worked out from the days you've checked in. Open the habit in the Habits tab to review it, or tell us what you're seeing.
